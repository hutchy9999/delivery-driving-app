Shift Profit privacy

Updated 27 October 2026

Your records

This personal delivery tool stores shifts, jobs, reviewed offer addresses, places, notes and optional location captures in your browser on this device. There is no Shift Profit account or central delivery database. Downloaded backups contain these records; keep them private. Clearing browser storage can delete them.

Screenshots and traffic

Screenshots are read locally. The app does not upload or retain screenshot images or full OCR transcripts. OCR software and language files download from jsDelivr when needed. Your original screenshot remains in your gallery.

If you explicitly enable live traffic, uploading an offer requests a short foreground location fix. Google Maps receives the current position and the pickup/drop-off postcodes used for routing, along with normal connection information such as your IP address and browser key. Postcodes identify approximate areas; exact customer names and full address text are not needed for these requests. Google’s returned travel times and distances stay in memory and are not added to job history or backups. No Google routes are drawn on the OpenStreetMap earnings map.

The browser API key stays in this device’s settings and is excluded from backups and the public repository. It is a browser credential protected by website/API restrictions, not a secret from someone who can inspect this browser. Turn off traffic and remove the key in Settings. No background GPS is collected.

Google’s processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Google Maps features are also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms.

Other external services

GitHub Pages hosts the app. Restaurant-name searches send the query/work area to Photon; postcode searches send postcodes to Postcodes.io and nearby venue searches to Overpass. Requested area postcode lookups also use Postcodes.io. OpenStreetMap tiles disclose the viewed map region and normal connection information to the tile service. Opening navigation sends the chosen destination to Google Maps. These services have their own terms and privacy practices.

You can export backups, edit recorded jobs and remove saved completion locations using the existing app controls. No automatic upload of your delivery history is performed.
